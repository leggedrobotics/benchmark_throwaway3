📜 Rules

  1. Beating baselines: To be eligible for any prizes or rewards, participants must beat the provided baselines for the challenge. Please refer to the Submission section for more details.
  2. Final evaluation: The final evaluation of the models will be done privately, on a private set of CAD models which will not be released to the public. This evaluation will determine the final rankings and winners. The test set from last year has been made public, but will not be used for the final evaluation.
  3. Data usage: Participants are not permitted to use any data other than the 3DCoMPaT++ dataset for training their models.
  4. Technical report: Each participant must submit a technical report detailing their methods to be eligible for any prizes or rewards; the report will be made public.

These rules aim to ensure fair competition and maintain the integrity of the challenge by preventing any unethical practices or unfair advantages.

🎖️ Awards

The first‑place winner will receive a prize of 1000 dollars, the second‑place winner will receive 500 dollars, and the third‑place winner will receive 250 dollars.

These prizes are designed to motivate participants to put their best effort into the challenge and to reward those who perform exceptionally well.
