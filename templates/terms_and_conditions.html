📜 Rules

  1. Beating baselines: To be eligible for prizes, participants must beat the provided baselines. See the “Submission” tab for details.
  2. Final evaluation: Models are evaluated privately on a hidden test set. Public test data will not be used for the final ranking.
  3. Data usage: You may only use the 3DCoMPaT++ data for training.
  4. Technical report: Each team must submit a report describing its method to qualify for awards.

These rules ensure fair competition and prevent unethical practices.

🏆 Awards

The first‑place winner will receive a prize of 1000 dollars, the second‑place winner will receive 500 dollars, and the third‑place winner will receive 250 dollars.

These prizes are intended to motivate participants to push state of the art in 3D computer vision.
